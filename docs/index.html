What it is

Just your structured ERG sessions.

OpenCycling does one thing and does it well: it drives your smart trainer's target power through a structured workout, samples every second, and lets you review the result. No avatars, no virtual worlds, no monthly fee — and everything stays on your machine.

ERG mode only — no SIM / free-ride / manual resistance

Everything you need for an ERG session

And nothing you don't.

📡

Automatic BLE scanning

Scans on launch with separate status for the trainer and the heart-rate monitor. The trainer is required to start; the HRM is optional.

📄

Zwift .zwo workouts

Reads workouts from a folder you choose. Supports Warmup, SteadyState, IntervalsT and Cooldown blocks, with target watts computed from your FTP.

🎯

ERG control per block

Sends each block's target power every second and follows linear ramps for warmups and cooldowns. A keep-alive resends the target so the trainer never drops resistance.

📊

Recording & history

Power, heart rate and cadence are stored every second in a local SQLite database. Browse past sessions and open any one for stats, a power graph and zone breakdowns.

A clean, focused desktop app

Light, fast, and out of your way.

Works with standard BLE devices

OpenCycling speaks standard FTMS (trainers) and HRS (heart rate), so any compliant device should work. Only the hardware below has been verified.

DeviceTypeProtocolStatus
Decathlon D500Smart trainerFTMS (0x2AD2)✓ Tested
Polar (H9 / H10 / OH1…)Heart rate monitorHRS (0x2A37)✓ Tested
Other FTMS trainersSmart trainerFTMS (0x2AD2)○ Should work
Other HRS strapsHeart rate monitorHRS (0x2A37)○ Should work

Requires FTMS Set Target Power. A trainer without it can't be used (no free-ride / slope mode). A heart-rate monitor is optional. Primary tested platform is Windows; devices are filtered by name prefix (D500 / Polar) because UUID filtering is unreliable on Windows.
